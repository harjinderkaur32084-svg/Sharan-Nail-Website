About Sharan Studio

Where artistry meets meticulous detail.

Welcome to Sharan Studio — a boutique beauty space built on professionalism, creativity and an obsession with the finer details. For over eight years we have turned brides, artists and everyday moments into lasting memories.

Every service uses certified, industry-leading products and sterilised tools, so your results are not only stunning but safe. From a pristine spa manicure to a full glam bridal look, our artists treat every appointment as a fresh canvas.

  • Certified & experienced beauty artists
  • Premium, cruelty-free product range
  • Sterilised tools & strict hygiene standards
  • Personalised consultations for every client
